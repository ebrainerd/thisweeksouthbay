# Subject: Happy Monday — Hometown Fair, a big Torrance Saturday, and last call for butterflies

**Preview text:** Last week (Sep 21–27) and this week (Sep 28–Oct 4) in the South Bay.

_Issue 3 · Monday, September 28, 2026 · Draft — not sent_

---

Hey.

Happy Monday. Here's what's on in the South Bay this week — short version so you can decide whether to leave the house.

If you’re new here: This Week South Bay is a nights-out board for Hermosa, Manhattan Beach, Redondo, El Segundo, Torrance, and Palos Verdes. Full times and links live at thisweeksouthbay.com.

## Last week (Sep 21–27)

The weekend went big and a little bit German. Manhattan Beach Food & Wine took over westdrift Friday and Saturday nights, Smog City ran its second free Smogtoberfest weekend in Torrance, and Oktoberfest popped up in the King Harbor lot in Redondo and at Mom Said Yes in Torrance. Sunday was car day: the 7th annual Downtown Manhattan Beach Car Show (an MBPD K-9 fundraiser) in the morning, then Rods, Rides & Relics on the Redondo pier until 3.

Elsewhere on the board: the El Segundo Main Street Fair Saturday, the Downtown Torrance Antique Street Faire Sunday, KYŌWA’s taiko at Armstrong Theatre, and opening weekend of *The Luckiest Woman Alive* at Second Story Theatre in Hermosa. Comedy ran Paul Reiser and Orny Adams midweek at CMC, the 10 Comics Show on the weekend, and Torrance Comedy Underground at Hermosa Brewing Co. Torrance on Friday. Deep End had Guy Mantzur Friday and The Walker Roaders Saturday; Lighthouse ran Flux Capacitors, DeadRinger, and Purple Generation. JCC South Bay hosted free Sukkot gatherings Friday night and Sunday afternoon, and the peninsula libraries stayed busy with *Hamnet*, *Coco*, the Livingston Ensemble, and a Kotoeda recital.

## This week (Sep 28–Oct 4)

Midweek is quiet. The weekend is not. Keep Saturday open.

Farmers markets do their usual rotation (MB Tuesday, North Redondo Wednesday, Redondo / Manhattan Village / El Segundo Thursday, Hermosa Friday, Riviera Village and Palos Verdes Sunday). Trivia with BUDDS is at Brewport Taphouse in El Segundo Tuesday 7pm (free to play). Deep End has Boots on the Pier with Cobra Cowboy Wednesday ($10.95–$12.15), and Dick The Citizen plays Lighthouse Thursday at 8:30.

**Manhattan Beach owns the weekend.** The Manhattan Beach Hometown Fair runs Saturday and Sunday, 10am–6pm, at Live Oak Park / Dorsey Field / Joslyn Center — free admission. Saturday starts early with the 49th annual MB 10K downtown (around 7:30am; free to watch, with live music and an expo at the finish), so give downtown a wide berth if you’re driving that morning. pages: a bookstore has a free Barney Saltzberg story time Saturday 9:30am and Carrie Talick (*The Alibi Club*) at 5pm. CBVA beach volleyball is on the Manhattan Pier / Strand courts Sunday from 9am, free to watch.

**Torrance takes Saturday night.** Halal Night Fest fills the Torrance Cultural Arts Center 4–11pm (free admission; food and a bazaar), and ScareDown in Downtown Torrance runs 4–9pm on Sartori Ave (free — games, contests, live bands, a DJ, trunk-or-treat, and a marketplace). Earlier in the day, *Unleashed: True Stories About the Animals We Love* is at Armstrong Theatre at noon ($25–$35). Mom Said Yes has LA Winter Friday and Petty or Not, a Tom Petty tribute, Saturday.

Music and comedy: Deep End has The Young Dubliners with Ockhams Razor Friday and Cubensis performing *American Beauty* Saturday (both $21.75–$48.15 — if you caught Cubensis free on the Hermosa sand on the 20th, this is the full-album version). Lighthouse runs the 90s Rock Show Friday (limited free tickets on Eventbrite), We Are The 80’s Saturday at 4pm, Dick The Citizen again Saturday at 8, and Motley Inc Sunday at 3. The 10 Comics Show is at CMC Friday and Saturday ($27). Howie Mandel Sunday is already sold out, so don’t go looking.

It’s closing weekend for *The Luckiest Woman Alive* at Second Story Theatre in Hermosa: Friday 8pm, Saturday 2pm and 6pm, Sunday 2pm (~$25–$40 / PWYC). Magic of the Jack O’Lanterns runs Thursday through Sunday at South Coast Botanic Garden (~$25.99 adults incl. fees; timed entry, so check the calendar for your night’s window).

**Palos Verdes this week:** Last call for SOAR tropical butterflies at South Coast Botanic Garden — it closes Sunday, Oct 4 (timed tickets; members $7, non-members up to $27 including garden admission). Aidan Sears Quartet plays a free album-release set at Malaga Cove Library Tuesday 3–6pm. The Friends book sale fills the Peninsula Center Library parking garage Friday through Sunday, 10am–4:30pm (free entry; books priced). Saturday is stacked: habitat restoration at Lunada Canyon / Agua Amarga 9am–noon (free, register, ages 16+), the PVPLC first-Saturday guided nature walk at George F Canyon at 10:30am (free, RSVP), and NYT-bestselling authors Elizabeth Letts and Melanie Benjamin at Peninsula Center Library 3–4:30pm. PV Art Center throws a free 95th Anniversary Open House Saturday and Sunday, 10am–4pm. PV Farmers Market is Sunday 8am–1pm on Silver Spur in RHE.

Full times, prices, and links: thisweeksouthbay.com (and /next-week for the early look).

Have a good week. Don’t let Monday convince you nothing interesting is happening within a ten-minute drive.

— This Week South Bay
